Add to Your Home Screen

Launch Wonderland Chronicles full-screen with its golden pocket-watch icon and crisp Read-Aloud narration.

🤖 Android Phone / Tablet (Chrome)

1. Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
3. Tap Add / Install to place the icon on your home screen.

🍎 iPad & iPhone (Safari)

1. Tap the Share icon (square with an upward arrow) in Safari.
2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
3. Tap Add in the upper-right corner.
